Is LEGO really getting more expensive?

We all seem to think so. I decided to look at the sets, the prices and the pieces to find out.

01 / THE TREND

How has the value changed?

This is the big one: what's the actual cost, in price per piece? Of course, you've a number of ways to choose - absolute value would seem the obvious one, but then we need to take into account inflation, and in the UK that gets complicated, with multiple indexes. We could compare it to the stock market, though that might cause issues around 2008. So let's choose a different measure as our default: how many pieces would you get for the cost of one Big Mac?

02 / POSSIBLE REASONS

Why do we all think it is?

So, in that case, why do we all think it is? Let's take a look at some possible reasons.

A THEME FAR FAR AWAY

What about the most popular theme?

In the past 25 years, just one licensed theme has had sets released every single year. Interestingly enough, for our analysis, it's also one of the very few themes where the absolute value of piece per pound (not adjusting for inflation), has, after staying steady for most of the 2010s decreased over the past five years.

AT THE TOP END

What do the priciest sets cost?

Here's an obvious one to look at, so let's take the average cost of the five most expensive sets released each year, and, oh boy. Although they've stayed largely steady for the past few years, they rocketed up from 2015 to 2021 and haven't really come down since (and they left the Big Mac behind a long time ago).
